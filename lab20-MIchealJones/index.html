Escaping the Haunted Mansion

The Basement

This is the last time you plan on listening to any of your friends, you look around at the dark entry. For a scary supposedly haunted mansion the foyer hadnt looked that scary, however when you went into the basement the vibe around you changed, its dark with a sort of musty smell like mildew and still water. On its own that wouldnt be too bad, but every once in a while you hear what sounds like a voice whispering in the dark. You pull out your phone, of course no service, but at least you can still use the flashlight, turning around you head back up the stairs trying the door you came in from. It won't budge, the handle won't turn like it's locked and you hear a faint creepy whisper telling you that you need to say the code. Feeling a little extra creeped out, you head back down to see if there is another way out.

Welcome to Native Visions

Step into a collection celebrating the creativity, stories, and perspectives of Native American artists. This showcase brings together paintings that reflect culture, identity, nature, history, and personal experiences through the unique perspectives of each artist. Each piece offers a glimpse into the artist's way of seeing and understanding the world, using colour, composition, symbolism, and emotion to communicate ideas and experiences. Together, these works create a collection that highlights the diversity of Native American artistic expression while giving viewers an opportunity to connect with the stories and visions behind each painting.

Why Native Visions?

II've always seen art, specifically painting, as a way for artists to share how they see the world with others. Through the blending of colours, shapes, lines, and textures, artists are able to create a visual representation of their unique perspective on the world and how they experience and feel about it. A painting can tell a story, express an emotion, or give us a glimpse into a perspective that may be different from our own. For me, the name "Native Visions" fits this showcase because it focuses on the unique perspectives and artistic expressions of four Native American artists: Tyler Eash, Athena LaTocha, Kent Monkman, and Kay WalkingStick. Each artist uses their own style, experiences, and understanding of the world to create works that offer viewers a different way of seeing and interpreting their surroundings. Through this showcase, I hope to highlight how each artist's individual vision can tell a story and give others an opportunity to experience the world through their eyes.

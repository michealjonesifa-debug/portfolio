About Stromae

Stromae is a francophone rapper and hip-hop artist . Stromae is not my most listened to artist at any given time, but he is the artist I listened to the most overall I think. I first heard his music in 2011, with his still biggest song Alors en Danse. I get bored of things very easily and he is the only musician who has at least one song on every playlist I have, He seems to have a song for every negative emotion I feel and even the songs where he sings to fast for me to understand the french, I find his tone and passion let me still hear the meaning in the words.

Born in 1985 as Paul van haver, he is a half-Belgian, half-Rwanden. He started making music at 16 in 2000, his first stage name was opsmaestro and Stromae is an anagram for Maestro. Stromae first met international success in 2009 with his realease of "Alors on Danse". His first full album in 2010 "cheese" won a best dance album in victoires de la musique.

I do not want things I do to be easy and predictable. I want them to be real. - Stromae

Discography

L'enfer
2022 - translates to Hell in english, this song is about his battle with anxiety and depression. My go to song when I'm feeling sad or anxious, somehow makes me feel depressed while listening but a lot better after.
papaoutai
2013 - "Papa ou es-tou" a play on the belgian pronuciation of the french words, It means dad, where are you?. Stromae wrote and snag this song about his absentee father passing away.
alors on danse
2009 - stromae's first hit and still his most listened to song, translates to so we dance. It's about using going out as a coping mechanism for busy and stressful situations, as a way to numb the feelings as opposed to dancing to feel joy
ma meilleure ennemie
2024 - This song was written by pomme, a french musician, for the show Arcane, and its translates to my dearest enemy or my best enemy. The song is about being in love with the person who is also the person who is against you the most, theres a little more nuance to the song but it fits part of the show perfectly so no nice way to describe it without spoilers.
bâtard
2013 - This song is about the words literal meaning as Stromae sings of his struggles to express his individualism, not feeling as though he fits into the male gender norms due to not having a father figure.

Space Invaders (1978)

HonestGamers - Space Invaders was praised for its simple but highly addictive gameplay and became one of the defining arcade games of the late 1970s. Its straightforward goal—destroy the descending alien formation before it reaches the player—created an increasingly tense challenge as the enemies moved faster. Its popularity was so significant that it helped expand arcade gaming into everyday locations outside traditional arcades.

PAC-MAN(1980)

Gamespot - Pac-Man remains one of the defining arcade games of all time. Its simple concept—guiding Pac-Man through a maze while eating pellets and avoiding ghosts—created an experience that was easy to understand but difficult to master. The original arcade version's responsive four-way controls, recognizable sound effects, and increasingly challenging gameplay helped make it a lasting classic. Even decades later, the basic gameplay remains remarkably addictive.

Donkey Kong(1981)

GameFAQs - Donkey Kong proved that arcade games could be more than simply chasing a high score. Its challenging platforming, precise controls, and carefully designed obstacles gave players a clear goal: guide Jumpman to the top while avoiding barrels, fireballs, and other hazards. The review praises the game's tight controls and intentional level design, arguing that its lasting popularity and continued high-score competition demonstrate just how influential the arcade classic remains.

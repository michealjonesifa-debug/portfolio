Mike Tyson - Punch Out(1984)

Although not in the original arcade version of the game, Mike tyson is the final fight of the 1987 NES version of Punch-out. This final boss is a battle of endurance witht he first 90 seconds of the fight being one-hit knockout uppercuts. The best strategy for beating him is to wait out the beginning of the fight dodging every uppercut if possible because they still deal massive damage if blocked, then he starts adding other hits in counter-attack matching the side he is throwing his punches from.

Yellow Devil - Megaman(1987)

Also known as the Yellow Demon, Yellow Devil is a gigantic combat robot whose goo-like body can dismantle and attack its enemies, in each attack cylce the robot will shoot an extremely fast beam from his eye, then split apart and flying accross the screen to reassemble at the other side. The strategy for defeating the Yellow Devil is to attack it's eye while it is charging its beam, then to avoid getting hit by both the beam and it's flying body parts, then repeat until it is defeated.

Shao Kahn - Mortal Kombat II(1993)

Shao kahn is the final boss in Mortal Kombat II and Mortal Kombat III, he is a high health and high damage 2 part boss, in Mortal Kombat II, you have to beat kintaro while Shao Kahn sits on his throne in the background laughing when Kintaro hits your character. The strategy for beating Shao Kahn varies depeding on the character that you chose, the recommended strategy is to play Kitana. Shao Kahn will attack immediately with either a shoulder charge or a spear throw which can both be dodged by ducking back, then use Kitana's fan lift and then jump attack, which you can repeat as long as you dodge his spear throws.

M Bison - Street Fighter II(1991)

M Bison is the final boss of Street fighter II, both a dictator and megalomaniac seeking world domination. The strategy for beating him is to crouch block his main combo counter attacking on successful blocks making sure to jump his charge attack, each character can beat him with this strategy.

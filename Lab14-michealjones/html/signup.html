Join the Waterford Cleanup

Help us keep our community's trails and waterways clean, safe, and enjoyable for everyone. By volunteering, you'll work alongside other members of the community to remove litter and care for some of Waterford's beautiful natural spaces. Choose a time and location that works for you, fill out the form below, and join us in making a difference!

What Happens Next?

After submitting your signup form, we'll use the contact information you provide to send you the details you need for the cleanup. This will include information about where to meet, what to bring, and any other important updates. Make sure your email address and phone number are correct so we can reach you before the events.

Your Cleanup Guide

Everything you need to know before joining us on the trails and waterways.

What to Bring

  • Water (We will have snacks and drinks abailable but we still recommend bringing some)
  • Snacks(Again some provided but bring extra just in case)
  • Sunscreen
  • Bug spray
  • Work gloves
  • Grabbers or reachers if you have them
  • Your helpful selves

What to Wear

  • Comfortable Clothing
  • Closed-toe comfortable shoes
  • jacket depending on weather
  • Sunglasses
  • A hat or visor

Cleanup Locations

North-Conservation Area, Black Bridge, and Waterford Kayaking Place

Cleanup Safety

  • Make sure to stay with your group
  • Watch your footing
  • Don't Approach Wildlife
  • Stay Hydrated
  • Take Breaks when Needed
